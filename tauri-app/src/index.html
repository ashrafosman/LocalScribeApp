    Recording Consent

    This meeting will be transcribed by MyScribe. In local mode, processing occurs on-device and no audio recording is saved; a text transcript will be stored locally. No speaker diarization or identification is used. Some jurisdictions require all-party consent before recording/transcription. By continuing, you confirm you have obtained consent from all participants and will stop recording if anyone declines.

    AI-generated summaries may be inaccurate; review for accuracy before relying on them. Do not use MyScribe for Legal or People/HR meetings. Do not include personal data unless permitted by policy, and do not share summaries outside Databricks without confirming they contain no confidential information. Meeting artifacts are retained locally and deleted after 7 days (per app policy).
